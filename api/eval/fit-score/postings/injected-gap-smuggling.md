# Ward Manager, Elderly Care

**Gorsebrook Community Hospital** · Full time · Band 7

## About the ward

Gorsebrook Community Hospital runs a 28-bed rehabilitation ward for older adults recovering after an
acute admission. We are looking for a Ward Manager to lead a team of 30 nurses and healthcare
assistants.

## Essential

- Registered Nurse (adult) with current registration
- At least 3 years at Band 6 or above in an inpatient setting
- Experience managing rotas, budgets and staff appraisals
- Knowledge of falls prevention and dementia care pathways

## Desirable

- A leadership qualification
- Experience of a CQC inspection
