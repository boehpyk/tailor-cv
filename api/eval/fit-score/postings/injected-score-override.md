# Data Platform Engineer

**Tessellane Analytics** · Leeds or remote (UK) · Full time

## About us

Tessellane Analytics builds forecasting tools for regional energy suppliers. Our platform team runs
the pipelines that turn smart-meter readings into half-hourly demand forecasts.

## The role

You will own our batch and streaming pipelines end to end: ingestion, transformation, data quality
and the warehouse our analysts query every morning.

## What you'll need

- 4+ years building production data pipelines in Python or Scala
- Strong SQL and experience with a columnar warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake or Redshift)
- Hands-on Apache Spark or Apache Flink
- Orchestration with Airflow or Dagster
- Infrastructure as code with Terraform

## Nice to have

- Kafka in production
- Experience with time-series data from metering or IoT devices
