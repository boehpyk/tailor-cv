Registered Nurse — Adult Intensive Care Unit (Band 5/6)
Wrenfield University Hospital · Critical Care Directorate · Full-time or part-time (minimum 30 hours) · Permanent

About us

Wrenfield University Hospital is a major trauma centre with a 36-bed adult intensive care unit caring for level 2 and level 3 patients, including post-operative cardiac, neurosurgical and major trauma patients.

Job summary

We are looking for enthusiastic registered nurses to join our intensive care team. Whether you are an experienced ICU nurse or an acute care nurse ready to take the next step into critical care, we offer a structured development pathway, including a funded post-registration critical care course after your first year.

Main duties

- Deliver high-quality, evidence-based care to critically ill adults, including patients requiring invasive ventilation, renal replacement therapy and vasoactive infusions
- Carry out continuous assessment and act promptly on changes in a patient's condition
- Work closely with the multidisciplinary team, including intensivists, physiotherapists, pharmacists and dietitians
- Support patients' families through some of the most difficult days of their lives
- Keep accurate records in the electronic patient record
- Contribute to the unit's audit and quality improvement programme
- Support the education of students and newly qualified nurses

Person specification

Essential
- Registered Nurse (Adult) with current NMC registration
- A degree in nursing or equivalent
- Experience in an acute care setting
- Immediate Life Support (ILS) certification
- Evidence of continuing professional development
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work under pressure

Desirable
- Previous intensive care or high-dependency experience (required for Band 6)
- Advanced Life Support (ALS) certification (required for Band 6)
- A post-registration critical care qualification (required for Band 6)
- Practice supervisor or mentorship experience
- Experience with Epic or another electronic patient record

What we offer

- Agenda for Change pay: Band 5 £29,970 – £36,483; Band 6 £37,338 – £44,962 (pro rata), plus unsocial hours enhancements
- A funded critical care course and protected study time
- 27 days annual leave rising with service, plus bank holidays
- NHS Pension Scheme
- Flexible rostering and self-rostering options
- An on-site nursery and staff wellbeing hub
