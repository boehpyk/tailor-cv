About Aurelian

Aurelian Care Technologies builds software that helps hospitals discharge patients safely and on time. Every day in the UK, thousands of patients who are medically fit to leave hospital stay in a bed because the arrangements for their care at home, in a care home or in a community setting are not ready. Each of those delays is bad for the patient, who loses mobility and independence with every extra day, and bad for the hospital, which cannot admit the next person waiting in the emergency department. Our platform connects hospital discharge teams, community health services, social care and care providers around a single, shared picture of each patient's discharge, so that the work happens in parallel rather than one phone call at a time.

Our product today

Aurelian is used by 31 NHS trusts and more than 900 care providers. The platform includes a discharge coordination workspace for hospital teams, a referral and capacity marketplace for care providers, analytics for system leaders, and integrations with the major electronic patient record systems used in UK hospitals. Our clinical decision support module, which predicts which patients are likely to face a complex discharge, is registered as a medical device. We closed our Series C round last year and are now preparing to launch in two further markets, including the United States.

Why this role exists

Engineering at Aurelian has grown from 15 to 70 people in three years. We have excellent engineers and a strong product culture, but the practices that served a small team are starting to strain: releases need more coordination than they should, ownership of some services is unclear, and our medical device obligations are handled by a few heroic individuals rather than by the way we work. Our CTO, who has led engineering since the company was founded, is moving to focus on architecture, security and our machine-learning strategy. We are looking for a Vice President of Engineering to lead the engineering organisation through its next stage of growth.

How we work

Our engineering principles are simple, and we expect our leaders to model them:
- Patients first. Every technical decision is ultimately about a person waiting to go home.
- Small teams, clear ownership. Teams own their services from design to operation.
- Ship small, ship often. Trunk-based development, feature flags and automated testing are how we deliver safely.
- Write it down. Decisions are recorded, incidents are reviewed without blame, and knowledge does not live in one person's head.
- Compliance is engineering. Regulatory evidence is produced by the way we work, not assembled at the end.

We are a hybrid company with an office in London that most teams use once or twice a month. Around half of our engineers are fully remote within the UK.
