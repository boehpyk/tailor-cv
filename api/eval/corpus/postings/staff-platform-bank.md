Staff Platform Engineer
Lumenvale Bank · Edinburgh or London, hybrid (two days a week in the office) · Permanent

Lumenvale Bank is a digital bank for small businesses, regulated by the PRA and the FCA, with 310,000 customers and a platform team that treats reliability as a product.

About the role

As a Staff Platform Engineer you will set the technical direction for the platform that every one of our 40 product teams deploys onto. You will work across teams rather than within one, and you will be accountable for outcomes that span quarters: how we run Kubernetes, how services talk to each other, and how we keep a regulated bank available at 99.95%.

Responsibilities

- Own the technical strategy for our Kubernetes platform (Amazon EKS), across multiple clusters and regions
- Design and build internal platform components in Go: deployment tooling, service templates and admission controllers
- Lead the adoption of a service mesh and a zero-trust network model across more than 300 services
- Define service-level objectives with product teams and build the tooling that measures them
- Drive incident reviews for platform-related incidents and follow through on systemic fixes
- Mentor senior engineers and help grow the next generation of staff engineers
- Work with Security, Risk and Compliance at Lumenvale on operational resilience requirements (important business services and impact tolerances)

Requirements

- Extensive experience building and operating production Kubernetes clusters at scale
- Strong Go programming skills, including writing Kubernetes controllers or operators
- Infrastructure as code with Terraform, including designing modules used by many teams
- A deep understanding of networking: DNS, load balancing, TLS, and service meshes such as Istio or Linkerd
- Experience in a staff, principal or equivalent role, influencing technical direction across multiple teams
- Experience in financial services or another regulated industry

Desirable

- PCI DSS experience
- Contributions to open-source cloud-native projects
- Chaos engineering experience
- Python for tooling

Benefits

Salary £120,000 – £140,000, an annual bonus, share options, 30 days holiday, a 10% pension contribution, private medical and dental cover, and a £2,000 learning budget.

Our hiring process has four stages: a call with our talent team, a technical conversation about a system you have built, a system design session, and a values interview. We never ask for unpaid take-home work.

Lumenvale Bank is committed to building a diverse team. We are a Disability Confident employer and will offer an interview to disabled applicants who meet the essential criteria for the role.
