Product Marketing Manager
Stackhaven · Remote (UK or Ireland) · Full-time

Stackhaven makes inventory and order-management software for B2B wholesalers. Our customers are distributors selling to shops, restaurants and trade buyers, and we're the system their sales reps, warehouse teams and finance teams all live in.

We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to own how we position and launch our product to mid-market wholesalers.

What you'll do

- Own positioning and messaging for our core platform and two new add-on products
- Plan and run product launches with product, sales and customer success
- Build sales enablement material: pitch decks, battlecards, case studies and demo scripts
- Run win/loss interviews and competitor research, and turn them into something the sales team actually uses
- Work with demand generation on campaigns aimed at operations and finance buyers

What we're looking for

- 4+ years in product marketing, ideally for B2B SaaS
- Experience launching products and features with cross-functional teams
- A track record of creating sales enablement that sales teams adopt
- Excellent writing: clear, specific and free of buzzwords
- Comfort interviewing customers and prospects

Nice to have

- Experience marketing to wholesale, distribution, supply-chain or operations buyers
- Pricing and packaging experience

Salary £65,000 – £78,000, share options, 25 days holiday plus a winter shutdown, and a home office budget. Stackhaven is an equal opportunity employer.
