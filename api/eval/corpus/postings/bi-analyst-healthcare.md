Business Intelligence Analyst
Meridale Health Partners · Cardiff or remote (UK) · Full-time

Meridale Health Partners runs 45 private physiotherapy, diagnostic imaging and minor-procedure clinics. Our data team of six supports clinical operations, finance and marketing.

The opportunity

We're looking for a BI Analyst to own reporting for our clinical operations directors. You'll turn appointment, referral and utilisation data into dashboards that people actually use, and help us move from ad-hoc spreadsheets to a governed analytics model.

Key responsibilities

- Design, build and maintain Tableau dashboards for clinic utilisation, waiting times and referral conversion
- Develop and maintain data models in dbt on top of our Snowflake warehouse
- Partner with operations leaders to define KPIs, and make sure everyone uses the same definitions
- Investigate anomalies and present insights to senior stakeholders, including the executive team
- Document data sources, definitions and known data-quality issues
- Help train colleagues to self-serve in Tableau

Essential skills and experience

- At least 3 years of experience in a BI or data analyst role
- Advanced SQL, including window functions and performance tuning
- Proven experience building dashboards in Tableau
- Hands-on experience with dbt or a similar transformation tool
- Experience presenting to senior, non-technical audiences
- An understanding of data protection when working with special category (health) data

Desirable

- Experience in healthcare or another regulated sector
- Python for analysis
- Experience with Snowflake
- Statistics beyond descriptive reporting, such as forecasting or experimentation

What we offer

- Salary £42,000 – £50,000
- 27 days holiday plus bank holidays
- Free physiotherapy at any of our clinics
- An enhanced pension and life assurance
- Flexible hours around core hours of 10:00 – 15:00

Apply with your CV and a short note about a dashboard you are proud of. Meridale Health Partners is an equal opportunities employer, and we are happy to discuss flexible working and reasonable adjustments.
