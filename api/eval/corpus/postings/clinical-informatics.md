Clinical Informatics Specialist (Nursing)
Brackenmoor Care Network · Digital Services · Hybrid: Leeds office and hospital sites · £37,000 – £45,000

Who we are

Brackenmoor Care Network is a group of four hospitals and 20 community services in the north of England. We are two years into a programme to replace paper and legacy systems with a single electronic patient record (EPR), and our clinicians are at the centre of how it is designed.

About the role

Clinical informatics specialists sit between the wards and the technology. You'll bring a nurse's understanding of how care is really delivered to the design, build, testing and optimisation of our EPR, making sure it is safe, usable, and saves clinicians time rather than costing it.

What you'll be doing

- Gather and analyse clinical workflow requirements from nursing and allied health teams
- Work with the EPR build team to design documentation, order sets and nursing assessments
- Plan and run user acceptance testing with frontline staff
- Contribute to clinical safety hazard assessments for system changes, working with our Chief Clinical Information Officer and Clinical Safety Officer
- Design and deliver training for staff, from classroom sessions to at-the-elbow support at go-live
- Analyse EPR usage data and incident reports to find and fix safety or usability problems
- Champion digital change across Brackenmoor

What you'll bring

Essential:
- Registered nurse or allied health professional with current registration
- At least three years of post-registration clinical experience
- Hands-on experience of using an electronic patient record in clinical practice
- Experience of training or supporting colleagues with technology
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent written communication, including clear process documentation

Desirable:
- Previous experience in an informatics, EPR or digital transformation role
- Super-user or go-live support experience with a major EPR system
- Knowledge of clinical safety standards (DCB0129 and DCB0160)
- A qualification in health informatics, project management (for example PRINCE2) or business analysis
- Experience of quality improvement methodology

We offer hybrid working, 27 days leave plus bank holidays, the NHS Pension Scheme, a salary sacrifice cycle scheme, and a funded informatics qualification after probation.

Brackenmoor Care Network is committed to equality, diversity and inclusion. We particularly welcome applications from groups under-represented in digital roles in health and care.
