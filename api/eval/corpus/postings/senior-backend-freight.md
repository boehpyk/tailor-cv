Senior Backend Engineer (Python)
Cartwheel Freight Cloud · Manchester or remote within the UK · Full-time

About us

Cartwheel Freight Cloud is a transport management platform for mid-sized freight forwarders and hauliers. Our customers use Cartwheel to quote jobs, plan loads, track consignments and send invoices, and more than 1,900 companies across the UK and Ireland now run their working day on it. We are a team of 85, profitable, and growing steadily.

The role

Our Shipments team owns the services behind consignment tracking and customer notifications. Volumes have tripled in two years, and some of the decisions that got us here are starting to creak. We are looking for a senior backend engineer to help us scale these services, raise the bar on reliability, and mentor the engineers around them.

What you'll do

- Design, build and operate Python services (FastAPI, Celery) backed by PostgreSQL and Redis
- Lead the redesign of our tracking-event ingestion, currently a cron-driven batch job, into a streaming pipeline
- Improve the reliability of customer-facing webhooks and notifications: retries, idempotency and observability
- Write design documents and lead technical discussions within the team
- Take part in a fair on-call rota (currently one week in seven, with a paid allowance)
- Mentor mid-level engineers through code review and pairing

What we're looking for

- Significant professional experience building backend services in Python
- Strong PostgreSQL skills: schema design, query tuning, partitioning, and safe migrations on large tables
- Experience with asynchronous processing (queues, workers, event streams) and the failure modes that come with it
- Experience running services in production on AWS, including monitoring and incident response
- A habit of writing things down: design docs, runbooks, post-incident reviews
- Clear communication with product managers and customer support

Nice to have

- Experience with logistics, freight or delivery software
- Go or another compiled language
- Terraform
- OpenTelemetry

What we offer

- £75,000 – £90,000 depending on experience
- 28 days holiday plus bank holidays, and your birthday off
- 6% employer pension contribution
- Hybrid working from our Manchester office, or fully remote within the UK
- £1,000 annual learning budget and a conference ticket every year
