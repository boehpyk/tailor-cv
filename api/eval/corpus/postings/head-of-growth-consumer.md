Head of Growth
Pocketfern · London (hybrid, two days a week) · Full-time

Pocketfern is the plant-care app that tells you when to water, what's wrong with that yellowing leaf, and where to buy the pot that fits. We have 2.3 million downloads, 140,000 paying subscribers, and a Series A behind us.

The role

We are hiring our first Head of Growth to own subscriber acquisition, activation and retention. You'll lead a team of five (performance marketing, lifecycle, and a growth analyst), set the growth strategy with our founders, and be accountable for subscriber growth and payback.

You will

- Own the growth model and its targets: acquisition, trial-to-paid conversion, retention and lifetime value
- Lead paid acquisition across Meta, TikTok, Apple Search Ads and Google App Campaigns, with a budget of around £3 million a year
- Build our experimentation programme across the app, onboarding, paywall and pricing, together with product and design
- Develop lifecycle marketing across push, email and in-app messaging
- Grow and manage a team of five, and hire two more people in the next year
- Report on growth performance to the founders and the board

You have

- 7+ years in growth or performance marketing, with a strong track record in subscription products
- Experience leading a team, including managing managers or senior specialists
- Mobile app growth experience: app store optimisation, attribution with a mobile measurement partner such as AppsFlyer or Adjust, and SKAdNetwork
- Deep experience with experimentation, and the statistics behind it
- Comfort with SQL and analytics tools; you don't wait for an analyst to answer a question
- Ownership of a budget of £2 million or more

Bonus points

- Experience in consumer apps or e-commerce
- Pricing and paywall optimisation
- Community-led growth or content marketing

Why Pocketfern
