Junior Data Analyst
Hollis & Grange Stores · Bristol (hybrid) · Full-time, permanent · £30,000 – £34,000

Hollis & Grange is a family-owned chain of 64 convenience and neighbourhood stores across the South West. We're growing our small insights team and looking for a junior analyst who wants to learn how retail really works.

What you'll do

- Build and maintain weekly sales, waste and availability reports
- Write SQL to answer questions from the store operations, buying and marketing teams
- Help move our remaining Excel reporting into Power BI
- Look for patterns in promotions and seasonal sales, and explain what you find in plain English

What we're looking for

- A degree in a numerate subject, or equivalent experience
- Good SQL and confident Excel
- Some experience with Python or R
- Curiosity, attention to detail, and the ability to explain numbers to non-specialists

Nice to have

- Power BI or another dashboarding tool
- Any experience in retail, even part-time

Benefits include 25 days holiday, a 15% staff discount, a pension, and paid volunteering days. To apply, send your CV to jobs@hollisandgrange.example by the end of the month.
