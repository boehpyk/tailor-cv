UX Researcher
Quillbright Learning · Birmingham or remote (UK) · Full-time, permanent

About Quillbright

Quillbright Learning makes homework and revision software used by 1,100 secondary schools. Teachers set work, students practise, and parents see progress, and each of those three groups needs something different from us.

About the role

We're growing our product design team and hiring a UX Researcher to help us understand students, teachers and parents better. You'll plan and run research across the product, share what you learn in a way that changes decisions, and help product teams do good research of their own.

What you'll do

- Plan and conduct generative and evaluative research: interviews, contextual inquiry, usability testing, diary studies and surveys
- Research with young people safely and ethically, following our safeguarding and consent processes
- Synthesise findings and communicate them clearly to product managers, designers and engineers
- Maintain our research repository in Dovetail
- Combine qualitative insight with product analytics to decide where to focus
- Contribute to accessibility research with students who use assistive technology

What we're looking for

- 2+ years of professional experience as a UX researcher, or in a similar research role within a product team
- Experience with a range of qualitative and quantitative research methods
- Strong synthesis skills and the ability to tell a clear story with evidence
- Experience working closely with designers and product managers in an agile team
- A portfolio or case studies showing your research process and its impact

Nice to have

- Experience in education or with young users
- Accessibility research experience
- SQL, or experience with product analytics tools such as Amplitude
- Experience recruiting research participants

What we offer

- £48,000 – £58,000
- 28 days holiday plus bank holidays, and an extra day off at the end of each school term
- 5% pension contribution
- £1,000 learning budget
- An enhanced DBS check, paid for by us

To apply, send your CV and a link to your portfolio to talent@quillbright.example. Quillbright Learning is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children, and all appointments are subject to satisfactory checks.
